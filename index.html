  • DFPA to Begin Issuing Permits for Prescribed Pasture Burns
    DFPA will begin issuing permits for prescribed pasture burns as conditions allow. Landowners must meet safety requirements and receive approval before burning. Backyard debris burning remains restricted.
    Umpqua National Forest Launches Online System for Free Firewood Permits
    Umpqua National Forest visitors can now obtain free personal-use firewood permits online and print them from home. Each permit allows the collection of up to 10 cords per year.
    Sunrise Enterprises to Celebrate Opening of New Roseburg Facility
    Sunrise Enterprises will celebrate the opening of its new Mulholland Drive facility in Roseburg on Friday, Sept. 18. The expanded location will support Day Support Activities and other community-based services for people with disabilities.
    I-5 Delays Expected South of Canyonville During Hazard Tree Removal
    Drivers should expect weekday delays of up to 20 minutes on I-5 south of Canyonville beginning Sept. 14 as crews remove fire-damaged trees.
    Umpqua National Forest Lifts Public Use Restrictions Following Rainfall
    Umpqua National Forest will lower its fire danger level from High to Moderate and lift Stage 2 Public Use Restrictions beginning Sept. 11. Campfires in wilderness and dispersed areas will again be allowed, along with personal chainsaw use.
    West Coast Game Park Safari Owner Sentenced to 7½ Years in Prison
    West Coast Game Park Safari owner Brian Tenney was sentenced to 7½ years in prison after pleading guilty to 47 criminal charges, including 43 counts of animal neglect. He was also ordered to pay more than $340,000 in restitution.
    Roseburg Could See Its Last 90-Degree Day Wednesday
    Roseburg could reach 93 degrees Wednesday before temperatures cool sharply through the weekend, potentially making it the city’s final 90-degree day of the year.
    Riddle Elementary Closed for Two Days Due to Continued Bat Activity
    Riddle Elementary School is closed Wednesday and Thursday as the district works with health and wildlife officials to address continued bat activity inside the building.
